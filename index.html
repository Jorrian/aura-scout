Questions, answered plainly.

Is the iPhone Duo update available?

Aura Scout is preparing an upcoming iPhone Duo navigation update, using native navigation that adapts as available space changes. The app remains a local release candidate; release and device acceptance are still pending. Focused development checks do not replace physical-device and accessibility acceptance or release approval.

Does All Canada mean nationwide job coverage?

No. All Canada browses the loaded, reviewed catalogue. As of October 4, 2026, the approved pilot contains six Alberta projects from two sources and zero opportunity records. An empty region means a catalogue gap, not an absence of work.

Can I download Aura Scout?

Aura Scout remains a local release candidate for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. It is not available through the App Store or a public TestFlight beta. Genuine OS 26 device and accessibility acceptance remain required before external distribution.

Is Aura Scout a job board?

No. Aura Scout is project intelligence. It helps you follow sourced changes and understand relationships before you open the official page.

Does Aura Scout apply for me?

No. Applications remain between you and the employer. Aura Scout provides a private, manual tracker for your own stages.

Where does the information come from?

Every catalogue fact must point to a registered source with review status, observation time, verification time, and attribution.

Where is my information stored?

The local candidate stores your profile, saved items, tracker stages, and credential metadata on your device. There is no Aura Scout account or cloud sync.
