Methodology

The usefulness of Aura Scout depends on making evidence boundaries visible. This is how sources, relationships, matches, changes, and corrections are treated.

What Aura Scout is

Aura Scout is project intelligence for experienced construction and industrial workers. It organizes reviewed changes around projects, organizations, opportunities, requirements, and official sources.

It is not a general job board, recruiter, employer representative, dispatch hall, resume service, or application-submission tool.

The source gate

Source use begins denied. A source needs explicit decisions for:

  1. commercial reuse;
  2. access method, including whether automation is allowed;
  3. permitted fields; and
  4. required attribution.

A pending, blocked, or incomplete decision prevents catalogue approval. Manual review does not create permission to automate a source.

Human review and provenance

Every approved record requires:

  • a stable identifier and record type;
  • a registered source and canonical HTTPS link;
  • separate original and reviewed values;
  • observation and verification times;
  • relationship evidence and review status; and
  • an auditable correction history.

Aura Scout writes its own concise factual summary and links to the official page. It does not republish full job descriptions or source articles.

Relationship confidence

Aura Scout limits relationship language to four evidence states:

Employer confirmed
The employer's recruitment source explicitly names the project.
Project partner confirmed
The organization is connected to the project, but the job assignment is not stated.
Possible match
Location, timing, or wording suggests a connection that still needs review.
Unassociated
No supported project relationship is asserted.

General participation never proves that a particular vacancy belongs to a project.

How matching works

Matching is deterministic. It uses work-relevant information such as trade, region or supported distance, follows, and mobility preferences. The reason for each match is shown. Browse projects lets you explore independently of personal matching.

Matching never uses age, sex, family status, disability, race, religion, or another protected trait. Aura Scout does not invent opportunities or use AI to publish catalogue content.

Geography and catalogue coverage

Profiles and browsing support all ten provinces and three territories. Geography filters are free and apply only to the loaded, reviewed catalogue. Counts distinguish projects from opportunity records; they do not measure all work in a region.

Region means the sourced project location or opportunity worksite, not a recruiter’s office. Unknown locations are not assigned a province by guesswork. A project spanning multiple jurisdictions retains one identity and counts once in All Canada.

Choosing a province does not establish a coordinate origin or prove travel fit. Distance matching remains limited to supported local origins and reviewed coordinates. Regional studies are not individual construction projects or hiring evidence.

Changes, removals, and outages

Opportunities may move through discovered, active, changed, closing soon, expired, removed, unavailable, reopened, or corrected states.

A source outage does not count as evidence that an opportunity disappeared. Without an explicit source status, removal requires three successful observations without the listing.

Corrections and ranking

A material correction returns a record to review and remains visible in change history. Corrected information is not silently substituted for the prior claim.

Employer payment cannot add a record, improve its organic ranking, increase relationship confidence, or relax source treatment. Trust decisions remain independent of revenue.

Current limitations

Aura Scout is a local release candidate. As of October 4, 2026, its approved pilot contains six Alberta projects from two sources and zero opportunity records. These are reviewed project signals, not verified jobs or current nationwide coverage.

All source connectors remain disabled. National research must pass source-rights, provenance, classification, and human review before catalogue inclusion. Alerts run after local catalogue import or refresh; faster server-driven delivery, production subscriptions, and public support are not active.

Always confirm critical details—project relationship, requirements, location, closing date, and application instructions—on the official page before acting.
