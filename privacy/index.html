Aura Scout

Privacy

Aura Scout is built around local worker-owned records and source transparency. This notice states what the current candidate does—and what it deliberately does not do.

Scope and product status

This notice describes the Aura Scout local release candidate and this public website. Aura Scout is not yet publicly available through the App Store. The notice will be reviewed again before any external beta or commercial release.

Aura Scout is designed for iPhone and iPad. It does not currently provide an Aura Scout account, cloud sync, employer portal, or customer web app.

Information stored on your device

The local candidate may store the following worker-owned information on your device:

  • trade family, role, experience level, province or territory, travel radius, mobility, and rotation preferences;
  • saved projects, organizations, trades, and change categories;
  • manual application records and stages;
  • credential names, issuers, dates, and reminder preferences; and
  • notification preferences and local subscription entitlement state.

Aura Scout does not ask for credential document images, resumes, identity documents, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone access, or protected-trait information.

Collection and transmission

The local candidate has no Aura Scout account, external analytics, advertising SDK, CloudKit sync, or developer-operated server. Worker-owned records remain on the device. Opening an official link does not submit your profile or application records.

Catalogue updates in the local candidate come from bundled or manually imported, reviewed files. The app does not crawl websites or upload your profile to match it on a server. Canada-wide geography filters do not enable source automation or cloud sync.

Apple services and notifications

Test subscriptions use Apple's StoreKit environment. Apple processes purchase information; Aura Scout does not receive or store payment-card details. The app receives only the transaction and entitlement information needed to determine whether Pro features are available.

Credential and catalogue reminders are scheduled as local notifications after you request them. The local candidate does not use remote push notifications.

Official links and external services

Official project and opportunity pages open in your system browser. Those websites operate under their own terms and privacy practices. Aura Scout does not control what an external website collects after you leave the app.

This website

This version of the website has no account, contact form, advertising, analytics, marketing pixel, or first-party cookie. It does not ask visitors to submit personal information.

The intended public host is GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs a visitor's IP address for security when a Pages site is visited. A private design preview may also be hosted through ChatGPT Sites under restricted access. Those providers may process operational information needed to host, secure, and debug their services.

Review the applicable provider notice: GitHub Privacy Statement and ChatGPT Sites Terms.

Your control and deletion

You can delete Aura Scout's worker-owned records from Settings using the complete local-data deletion control. Related pending local notifications are cancelled.

Deleting local app data does not erase Apple's purchase history or records held by an external website you chose to open. Those records are governed by the relevant provider.

Changes and questions

This notice will change if Aura Scout adds accounts, cloud services, analytics, production notifications, or another data practice. Material changes must be reflected here and in App Store privacy disclosures before release.

Public support intake is not open while Aura Scout remains a local release candidate. The Support page records the current support boundary and will be updated before external testing.
