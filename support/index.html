Support

Aura Scout is not yet distributed publicly. This page records the current support boundary and the help that will be available before external testing.

Current availability

Aura Scout is a local release candidate and is not available for public download, TestFlight testing, purchase, or employer use. This page will become the public support destination before an external beta begins.

There is no public account to create and no website sign-in. Do not send credential documents, resumes, application details, or other personal records through public channels.

Browsing and empty regions

Choose Browse projects to explore beyond your saved profile, or use personal matching for preference-based results. All Canada and all ten provinces and three territories are available without Pro. Your role and experience preferences can be edited in Settings.

The approved pilot contains six Alberta projects and zero opportunity records as of October 4, 2026. If your selected region has no reviewed records, choose All Canada or another region. A catalogue gap does not mean that no work exists there.

Opening an official source

Aura Scout opens official pages in the system browser. If a page is unavailable, check your connection and try again later. A source outage does not automatically remove the last reviewed record.

Confirm application instructions, closing dates, requirements, and project relationships on the official page before acting.

Notifications and reminders

Aura Scout requests notification permission only after you create an alert or reminder. You can change notification permission in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

The local candidate schedules reminders on the device. It does not currently use a production remote-notification service.

Test purchases and restore

Monthly and annual products currently exist only in Apple's local StoreKit test environment. They are not public subscriptions and do not create a real charge.

Production pricing, availability, cancellation handling, and support instructions will be reviewed before App Store release.

Deleting local data

Use Aura Scout Settings to delete all worker-owned local records. This removes your profile, saves, alerts, manual application records, credential metadata, and notification preferences, and cancels related pending reminders.

Apple purchase history and information held by an official website are outside this local deletion control.

Reporting a source concern

Public correction intake will open before external testing. A useful report will identify the Aura Scout record, provide the official URL, and explain the suspected issue without including personal information.

Corrections must return the record to review and remain visible in its audit history.

Accessibility

Aura Scout is designed for Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, increased contrast, Reduce Motion, and keyboard navigation on iPad. Recent iPhone and iPad OS 27 simulator reviews cover normal and maximum text, national browsing, profile retention, and watch controls. Genuine OS 26 device acceptance and hands-on VoiceOver, keyboard, and pointer verification remain release gates. Public issue intake will include accessibility reports when beta support opens.

Contact boundary

No public support address is represented as active during the local-candidate phase. Verified contact details and expected response times will be published here before Aura Scout is distributed to external testers.
